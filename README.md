## App Description

**Users:** Hobby birdwatchers and hikers who want to record wildlife 
they encounter and build up a personal record of sightings over time.

**Core task:** Logging a sighting — capturing the species, location, 
and photo in one place, and being able to look back at what was seen, 
where, and when.

**Data:** 
- Species (name, scientific name, basic info from iNaturalist)
- Sighting (species, location, date/time, photo, notes)
- Outing (optional grouping of sightings from one trip/walk)
Sighting links to Species; Sighting may optionally link to Outing.

**Out of scope:** 
1. Social/sharing features (no posting sightings to other users)
2. Offline species identification via image recognition (AI photo ID)
